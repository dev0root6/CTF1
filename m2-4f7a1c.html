    The intercepted note

    Security caught a note being passed between two employees on the night the files went missing. It's scrambled, but not very well.

    note_1014.txtIntercepted
    HMJHP YMJ GQFSP UFLJ

    This is a Caesar cipher: every letter was moved forward by the same number of places in the alphabet. With a shift of 3, A becomes D and B becomes E.

    Shift the letters back to read the message.

    Type the plain-text message exactly as it reads.
